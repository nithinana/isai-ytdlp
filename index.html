Streaming can be slow

The main server is shared by everyone, so playing songs directly can buffer or lag. For the best experience, download your music and play it offline.

  1. Import your playlistsLibrary → Import. Bring them in from Spotify or YouTube Music with a link.
  2. Download themOpen a playlist and tap the download button, or choose “Download all”.
  3. Play offlineDownloaded songs start instantly, with album art and lyrics, even with no signal.
